# PMS-RS Segmentation Foundation

Segmentation is projection-driven behavioral classification derived from:

- stay behavior
- booking behavior
- spending behavior
- cancellation behavior
- accompaniment behavior
- channel behavior

Segments are behavioral interpretations rather than operational entities.

Segments are:

- derived
- rebuildable
- recalculable
- versionable
- model-dependent

Segments may evolve independently from operational schema.

Examples:

- repeat guest
- high-value guest
- OTA-dependent guest
- family traveler
- business traveler
- long-stay guest
- cancellation-risk guest

Segmentation must not become operational authority.
